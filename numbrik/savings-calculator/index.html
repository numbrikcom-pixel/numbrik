Savings Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 13, 2026

Find out how much your savings will grow with regular deposits and interest, or flip it around and see how much you need to save each month to reach a goal, such as an emergency fund, a down payment or a car.

How much will my savings grow?

Each month the balance earns interest at the monthly equivalent of the APY and your deposit is added. $5,000 plus $300 a month at 4.25% APY grows to about $26,100 in 5 years, including about $3,100 of interest.

How to use the Savings Calculator

  1. Choose growth or goal.
  2. Enter your starting balance and either your monthly deposit or your goal.
  3. Enter the APY and how many years.
  4. Read the final balance and how much of it is interest, with a year-by-year table.

How it works

Savings accounts quote an annual percentage yield (APY), which already includes compounding. The calculator converts it to a monthly rate, (1 + APY)^(1/12) − 1, grows the balance each month and adds your deposit. In goal mode it solves the same formula backwards for the monthly deposit.

High-yield savings accounts and money market accounts change their rates with the market, so treat the APY as an estimate over long periods. Interest on savings is taxable as ordinary income in the US.

Formula

Monthly rate r = (1 + APY)^(1/12) − 1
Future value = P(1 + r)^n + d × ((1 + r)^n − 1) ÷ r
Deposit for a goal d = (goal − P(1 + r)^n) × r ÷ ((1 + r)^n − 1)

Example

Starting with $5,000 and adding $300 a month at 4.25% APY for 5 years gives about $26,132. You deposit $23,000, so about $3,132 is interest.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I keep in an emergency fund?

A common guideline is three to six months of essential expenses, kept somewhere safe and easy to reach, like a high-yield savings account.

What's the difference between APR and APY?

APY includes the effect of compounding and APR doesn't. Savings accounts quote APY, so you can compare them directly.

How often is savings interest compounded?

Most US savings accounts compound daily and pay monthly. Because the APY already includes compounding, the calculator gives the same result either way.

Is interest on savings taxed?

Yes, in the US it's taxed as ordinary income in the year it's paid. Your bank sends Form 1099-INT if you earn $10 or more.
