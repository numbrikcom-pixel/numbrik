BAC Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 9, 2026

Estimate your blood alcohol content (BAC) from what you've had to drink, your weight and how long you've been drinking, plus roughly how long until it's back to zero. It's a learning tool: never use it to decide whether you can drive.

How is blood alcohol content calculated?

The calculator uses the Widmark formula. It converts your drinks to grams of alcohol (a US standard drink holds 14 g), divides by your body weight times a body-water constant (about 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women), and subtracts the alcohol your liver has cleared: on average about 0.015% BAC per hour.

How to use the BAC Calculator

  1. Choose your sex and enter your body weight.
  2. Enter how many beers, glasses of wine and shots you've had.
  3. Enter the hours since your first drink.
  4. Read your estimated BAC and the time until it reaches 0.00%.

How it works

The calculator uses the Widmark formula. It converts your drinks to grams of alcohol (a US standard drink holds 14 g), divides by your body weight times a body-water constant (about 0.68 for men and 0.55 for women), and subtracts the alcohol your liver has cleared: on average about 0.015% BAC per hour. Coffee, cold showers and sleep don't speed that up.

Real BAC can be much higher than the estimate. Strong craft beers, large wine pours and mixed drinks often hold two or more standard drinks, and drinking on an empty stomach, medication, fatigue and health conditions all raise it. The only safe plan is not to drive after drinking.

Formula

Alcohol (g) = standard drinks × 14
BAC % = alcohol ÷ (body weight in g × r) × 100 − 0.015 × hours
r ≈ 0.68 (men), 0.55 (women)

Example

A 170 lb man has three beers over two hours: 42 g of alcohol ÷ (77,111 g × 0.68) × 100 = 0.080%, minus 0.03% for two hours = about 0.050%. A 140 lb woman drinking the same would be at about 0.090%, over the 0.08% limit.

Frequently asked questions

What is the legal BAC limit for driving?

0.08% in every US state except Utah, where it's 0.05%. Commercial drivers are limited to 0.04%, and drivers under 21 face zero-tolerance limits of 0.00–0.02%.

How long does it take to get alcohol out of your system?

BAC falls by about 0.015% an hour, so 0.08% takes over five hours to reach zero. Nothing speeds it up: not coffee, water, food after the fact or a cold shower.

How many drinks gets you to 0.08%?

For many men around 170 lb it's three to four drinks in an hour or two; for many women around 140 lb it's two to three. It varies a lot, which is why no number of drinks is safe for driving.

Is this calculator accurate?

It gives a rough average. Your real BAC depends on food, drink strength, medication, health and genetics. Only a breath or blood test measures it.
