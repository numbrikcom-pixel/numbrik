Invoice Generator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 18, 2026

Create a clean, professional invoice in a minute. Fill in your details and your client's, add line items, tax and discount, and download it as a PDF. It's free, needs no account, and everything stays in your browser.

How do I create an invoice for free?

Enter your details, your client's, an invoice number, dates and each item with quantity and price, then add tax or a discount. Numbrik's invoice generator totals it and saves it as a PDF, free and without sign-up.

How to use the Invoice Generator

  1. Enter your name or business and contact details under From, and your client's under Bill to.
  2. Set the invoice number, date, due date and currency.
  3. Add each item or service with its quantity and price. Use + Add item for more lines.
  4. Add tax or a discount if needed, plus payment terms in the notes.
  5. Click Download PDF / Print and choose Save as PDF.

How it works

Each line amount is quantity × price. The subtotal is the sum of the lines; a discount comes off the subtotal, and tax is charged on the amount after the discount. The total due is subtotal − discount + tax.

The invoice is built in your browser and never sent to a server, so client details stay private. Your last invoice is remembered on this device, which makes the next one quicker: change the number and items, and you're done.

Formula

Line amount = quantity × price
Subtotal = sum of line amounts
Discount = subtotal × discount %
Tax = (subtotal − discount) × tax %
Total due = subtotal − discount + tax

Example

A freelancer bills 10 hours of design at $50 and 1 month of hosting at $20: subtotal $520. With a 10% discount ($52) and 8% sales tax on the rest ($37.44), the total due is $505.44.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make an invoice for free?

Fill in your details, your client's, and the items above, then click Download PDF / Print and choose Save as PDF. There's no sign-up, watermark or limit.

Is my information saved or uploaded?

No. The invoice is created entirely in your browser. Your last invoice is remembered only on your own device so you can reuse it.

What should an invoice number look like?

Any unique, sequential code works, such as INV-001, INV-002 or 2026-014. Never reuse a number; it helps you and your client keep clean records.

Do I need to charge tax on my invoice?

It depends on where you are and what you sell. In the US, many services aren't subject to sales tax, but some states tax them. In the UK and EU, VAT applies once you're registered. Check your local rules or an accountant.
