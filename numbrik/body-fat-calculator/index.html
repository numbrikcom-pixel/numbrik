Body Fat Calculator

Estimate your body fat percentage with a tape measure, using the US Navy method, and see your fat and lean mass and how the result compares with common ranges.

Loading calculator…

How to use the Body Fat Calculator

  1. Choose units and sex.
  2. Measure your neck, waist and, for women, hips. Enter them with your height and weight.
  3. Read your body fat percentage, its category and your fat and lean mass.

How it works

The US Navy method estimates body fat from body circumferences and height. It was developed so that body fat could be checked without special equipment, and it is usually within a few percentage points of lab methods for most people. Measure in the morning, keep the tape level and take the average of two or three readings.

The categories come from the American Council on Exercise (ACE). Body fat is a better guide to health than weight alone, but it is still an estimate. Very muscular or very lean people may get less accurate results.

Formula

Men:    BF% = 86.010 × log₁₀(waist − neck) − 70.041 × log₁₀(height) + 36.76
Women:  BF% = 163.205 × log₁₀(waist + hip − neck) − 97.684 × log₁₀(height) − 78.387
(all measurements in inches)

Example

A man 69 in tall with a 15.5 in neck and a 34 in waist has about 17.0% body fat, in the fitness range. A woman 64 in tall with a 13 in neck, 30 in waist and 40 in hips has about 31.7%, in the average range.

Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the Navy method?

For most adults it lands within a few percentage points of lab tests, but tape placement matters. Take two or three readings and average them.

Where do I measure my waist?

Men measure at the navel. Women measure at the narrowest point, usually just above the navel.

What is a healthy body fat percentage?

The ACE fitness and average ranges are 14–24% for men and 21–31% for women.

Is body fat better than BMI?

It gives more detail because it separates fat from muscle. BMI is quicker but can't tell the two apart.
