Car Lease Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 11, 2026

Work out a monthly car lease payment the way dealers do: from the negotiated price, residual value, money factor, down payment, fees and sales tax. It shows each part of the payment so you can check a dealer's quote.

How is a car lease payment calculated?

Add the depreciation fee, (net capitalized cost − residual value) ÷ months, to the rent charge, (net capitalized cost + residual) × money factor, then add sales tax. The money factor × 2,400 equals the APR.

How to use the Car Lease Calculator

  1. Enter the MSRP and the price you negotiated.
  2. Add your down payment and trade-in, and any fees rolled into the lease.
  3. Enter the term, the residual percentage and the APR (or money factor × 2,400).
  4. Read the monthly payment and the total cost of the lease.

How it works

A lease payment has two parts. The depreciation fee pays for the value the car loses while you drive it: the net capitalized cost (price plus fees, minus down payment and trade-in) minus the residual value, divided by the number of months. The finance or rent charge is (capitalized cost + residual) × money factor. Sales tax is then added to the payment in most US states.

The money factor is just an interest rate in disguise: multiply it by 2,400 to get the APR. Residual values for 36-month leases are often 50–60% of MSRP. Negotiating the price down lowers the payment just like it does when buying, and a large down payment is risky on a lease, because it's usually lost if the car is stolen or written off.

Formula

Net cap cost = price + fees − down payment − trade-in
Residual = MSRP × residual %
Depreciation = (net cap cost − residual) ÷ months
Rent charge = (net cap cost + residual) × money factor
Money factor = APR ÷ 2,400

Example

A car with a $40,000 MSRP negotiated to $37,500, $995 in fees and $3,000 down, on a 36-month lease with a 58% residual and 6% APR (money factor 0.0025): depreciation is $341.53 and the rent charge $146.74, so the payment is $488.27, or $522.44 with 7% sales tax.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good money factor?

Multiply it by 2,400 to compare it with loan rates. 0.0025 is 6% APR. Anything close to the rate you'd get on a new-car loan is reasonable; ask the dealer for the buy rate, the lowest the lender allows.

Is it better to put money down on a lease?

Usually as little as possible. A down payment just prepays the lease, and if the car is stolen or totaled early, gap coverage pays the lender but you typically don't get the down payment back.

What is the residual value?

The car's predicted value at the end of the lease, set by the leasing company as a percentage of MSRP. A higher residual means a lower payment. It's also your buyout price.

Can I negotiate a lease?

Yes. The price (capitalized cost) and often the money factor and fees can be negotiated, just as when buying. The residual is set by the lender.
