Student Loan Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 11, 2026

Work out your monthly student loan payment and the total interest you'll pay, compare the 10-year standard plan with 20- and 25-year terms, and see how much an extra payment each month saves.

How do you calculate a student loan payment?

Use payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n), with r the monthly rate and n the months. $30,000 at 6.5% on the 10-year standard plan is about $341 a month and $10,900 of interest.

How to use the Student Loan Calculator

  1. Enter your loan balance and interest rate. For several loans, use the total and a weighted average rate.
  2. Enter the repayment term: 10 years is the federal standard plan.
  3. Optional: add an extra monthly payment.
  4. Read the payment, the total interest and how much sooner you'd be debt-free.

How it works

Fixed-rate student loans are repaid like other installment loans: equal monthly payments over the term, mostly interest at first and mostly principal later. The calculator uses the standard amortization formula and shows how the payment and total interest change with longer terms.

Longer terms lower the payment but can nearly triple the interest. Extra payments go straight to principal and cut the interest charged every month after. With federal loans, ask your servicer to apply extra payments to principal rather than to future payments. Income-driven repayment plans set the payment as a share of income instead, so use the official Loan Simulator at studentaid.gov for those.

Formula

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)^−n)
r = annual rate ÷ 12,  n = months
Total interest = payment × n − P

Example

A $30,000 balance at 6.5% on the 10-year standard plan costs $340.64 a month and $10,877 in interest. Stretching it to 25 years drops the payment to $202.56 but the interest rises to $30,769.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to pay off student loans?

The federal standard plan is 10 years. Extended plans run up to 25 years, and income-driven plans can last 20 to 25 years before any remaining balance is forgiven.

Should I pay off student loans early?

If your rate is higher than what you'd reasonably earn investing, extra payments are a guaranteed return. Build an emergency fund and get any employer retirement match first.

How is student loan interest calculated?

Most federal loans charge simple daily interest: balance × annual rate ÷ 365 each day. That works out to about balance × rate ÷ 12 a month, which is how this calculator models it.

Is student loan interest tax-deductible?

In the US you may deduct up to $2,500 a year of student loan interest, subject to income limits. Check the current IRS rules for your filing status.
