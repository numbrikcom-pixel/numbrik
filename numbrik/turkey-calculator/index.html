Turkey Calculator

Plan your Thanksgiving or Christmas turkey. Enter the number of guests or the turkey's weight and your dinner time to see how big a bird to buy, when to start thawing it, how long to roast it, and when it needs to go in the oven.

How to use the Turkey Calculator

  1. Enter how many people you're feeding, or the weight of the turkey you already have.
  2. Choose stuffed or unstuffed, and fridge or cold-water thawing.
  3. Enter when you want to eat. The timeline shows when to start thawing and when the turkey goes in the oven.

How it works

Roasting times follow the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service chart for a fully thawed turkey in a 325°F (163°C) oven. They're a guide: ovens vary, so start checking an hour before the shortest time. The turkey is done when a food thermometer reads 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the breast, the innermost part of the thigh and the innermost part of the wing, and in the centre of any stuffing.

For thawing, the USDA allows about 24 hours in the fridge for every 4 to 5 pounds, or 30 minutes per pound in cold water, changing the water every 30 minutes and cooking the turkey straight away afterwards. Never thaw a turkey on the counter. Plan on 1 to 1½ pounds of whole turkey per person; the larger amount leaves leftovers. The timeline adds 30 minutes for the turkey to rest before carving.

Formula

Size: guests × 1–1.5 lb
Fridge thaw: 1 day per 4–5 lb
Cold-water thaw: 30 min per lb
Done at 165°F (74°C) · rest 20–30 min

Example

For 12 guests with leftovers, buy a turkey of about 12 × 1.5 = 18 lb. Thawing it in the fridge takes 4 to 5 days, so for Thursday dinner put it in the fridge by Saturday or Sunday. Unstuffed, an 18 lb turkey roasts for about 4¼ to 4½ hours at 325°F, so for dinner at 5:00 PM it goes in the oven at about 12:00 PM.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to cook a turkey?

At 325°F an unstuffed turkey takes roughly 13–15 minutes per pound: about 3 hours for 12 lb and 4¼–4½ hours for 18–20 lb. Always check it reaches 165°F.

How long does a turkey take to thaw?

About 1 day in the fridge for every 4–5 lb, so 4–5 days for a 20 lb bird. In cold water, 30 minutes per pound.

What temperature should a turkey be cooked to?

165°F (74°C), measured in the thickest part of the breast, the inner thigh and the wing, and in the centre of the stuffing.

Can I cook a turkey that's still frozen?

Yes, it's safe, but it takes about 50% longer, and you can't remove the giblets until it has partly cooked.

How big a turkey do I need?

About 1 lb per person for one meal, or 1½ lb per person if you want leftovers.
