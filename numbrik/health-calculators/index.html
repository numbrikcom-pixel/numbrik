Health Calculators

Health and fitness calculators for adults: body mass index with the World Health Organization categories, BMR and daily calorie needs from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, protein and macro targets, one rep max, running pace and bedtimes that line up with 90-minute sleep cycles. Each page explains its method and limits. Results are estimates, not medical advice.

Which one do I need?

BMI Calculator
Calculate body mass index from your height and weight in feet and pounds or centimetres and kilograms, and see the healthy weight range for your height.
Calorie Calculator
How many calories should you eat a day? Enter your age, sex, height, weight and activity level to get your maintenance calories, plus daily targets to lose or gain ½, 1 or 2 pounds a week. Charts below show typical needs by age and how many calories common activities burn.
Sleep Calculator
Find bedtimes and wake-up times that line up with 90-minute sleep cycles, so you are more likely to wake at the end of a cycle than in the middle of deep sleep.
Due Date Calculator
Estimate a baby's due date from the first day of your last period, the conception date or an IVF transfer date, and see how far along you are and when each trimester ends.
Body Fat Calculator
Estimate your body fat percentage with a tape measure, using the US Navy method, and see your fat and lean mass and how the result compares with common ranges.
BMR Calculator
Your basal metabolic rate (BMR) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest to keep you alive: breathing, circulation, temperature and cell repair. Enter your sex, age, height and weight to see your BMR and the calories you burn each day at different activity levels.
Protein Calculator
Work out how many grams of protein to eat each day based on your weight and goal, from the minimum for general health to the higher amounts used for building muscle or keeping it while losing fat.
One Rep Max Calculator
Estimate the most weight you could lift for a single rep (your one rep max, or 1RM) from a set you've already done, without having to test a true max. Works for bench press, squat, deadlift and other lifts.
Macro Calculator
Split your daily calorie target into grams of carbohydrate, protein and fat. Pick a balanced, high-protein, low-carb or keto split, or enter your own percentages.
Pace Calculator
Enter how far you ran and how long it took to get your pace per mile and per kilometre, your speed, and the finish time for a 5K, 10K, half marathon and marathon at the same pace.
Water Intake Calculator
Estimate how much water to drink each day based on your body weight and how much you exercise. Results are shown in ounces, cups and litres, alongside the official adequate intake for adults.
TDEE Calculator
Your TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) is how many calories you burn in a day, including exercise. Eat that many to keep your weight steady, fewer to lose weight, or more to gain. Enter your details and activity level to get yours.
Ideal Weight Calculator
Find a healthy target weight for your height. The calculator gives your ideal body weight from four medical formulas and the weight range that puts you in the healthy BMI band of 18.5 to 24.9.
Period & Ovulation Calculator
Enter the first day of your last period and your usual cycle length to predict your next period, your ovulation day and your most fertile days, for the next six months.
Height Calculator
How tall will your child be? Enter the mother's and father's heights to predict a boy's or girl's adult height with the mid-parental method doctors use, or use the toddler doubling rule.
Calorie Deficit Calculator
Find out how many calories to eat each day to lose weight, and roughly when you'll hit your goal. The calculator works out your maintenance calories, then subtracts a deficit for the pace you choose.
BAC Calculator
Estimate your blood alcohol content (BAC) from what you've had to drink, your weight and how long you've been drinking, plus roughly how long until it's back to zero. It's a learning tool: never use it to decide whether you can drive.
