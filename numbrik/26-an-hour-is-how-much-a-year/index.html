$26 an hour is how much a year?

How it's calculated

A full-time year is usually counted as 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 paid hours. $26 × 2,080 = $54,080. Paid holidays and paid vacation still count. If you take two weeks of unpaid leave, you work 50 weeks and earn $52,000.

A raise of $1 an hour adds $2,080 a year at full time. $26 an hour is 3.6 times the US federal minimum wage of $7.25. Many states and cities set a higher minimum.

$26 an hour after taxes

For a single filer in 2026 with no state income tax, $54,080 a year means about $4,310 in federal income tax, $3,353 in Social Security and $784 in Medicare. That leaves roughly $45,633 a year, $3,802.77 a month or $1,755.13 every two weeks. Use the take-home pay calculator to add state tax and 401(k) contributions. The pre-tax figures above apply to any currency paid by the hour, such as Canadian or Australian dollars; the after-tax estimate uses US rules.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $26 an hour every two weeks?

$2,080.00 before tax, at 40 hours a week. After estimated 2026 US federal tax and FICA for a single filer with no state tax, it is about $1,755.13.

How much is $26 an hour after taxes?

About $45,633 a year, or $3,802.77 a month, for a single filer in a state with no income tax in 2026. State and local taxes, retirement contributions and benefits lower this.

How much is $26 an hour part-time?

At 20 hours a week it is $520.00 a week, or $27,040 a year before tax. At 30 hours a week it is $40,560 a year.
