Rounding Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 9, 2026

Round a number to the nearest whole number, ten, hundred, tenth, hundredth or thousandth, and see it rounded to every place at once. You can also pick the rounding rule: half up, banker's rounding, always up, always down or truncate.

How do you round a number?

To round, look at the digit just to the right of the place you're rounding to. If it's 5 or more, round up; if it's 4 or less, keep the digit and drop the rest.

How to use the Rounding Calculator

  1. Type the number to round.
  2. Choose the place to round to.
  3. Choose the rounding method (half up is what schools teach).
  4. Read the result, plus the number rounded to every other place.

How it works

To round, look at the digit just to the right of the place you're rounding to. If it's 5 or more, round up; if it's 4 or less, keep the digit and drop the rest. 3.14159 to the nearest hundredth: the hundredths digit is 4 and the next digit is 1, so it becomes 3.14. To the nearest tenth, the next digit is 4, so it's 3.1.

Half to even (banker's rounding) sends exact halves to the nearest even digit, so 2.5 becomes 2 and 3.5 becomes 4. It avoids an upward bias when you round lots of numbers, which is why accountants and many computer languages use it. Ceiling always rounds up, floor always rounds down, and truncating just chops off the extra digits.

Formula

Look at the next digit to the right:
0–4 → round down (keep the digit)
5–9 → round up (add 1 to the digit)

Example

2,746.385 rounds to 2,746.39 (hundredths), 2,746.4 (tenths), 2,746 (whole number), 2,750 (tens) and 2,700 (hundreds). With banker's rounding, 2,746.385 to the hundredth still gives 2,746.38, because 8 is even.

Frequently asked questions

How do you round to the nearest tenth?

Look at the hundredths digit (the second after the decimal point). If it's 5 or more, add 1 to the tenths digit; otherwise leave it. 4.36 rounds to 4.4 and 4.34 rounds to 4.3.

What is 0.5 rounded to the nearest whole number?

1 with the usual half-up rule. With banker's rounding it's 0, because 0 is the nearest even number.

How do you round negative numbers?

Half up here means half away from zero, so −2.5 becomes −3. Floor always goes down, so −2.1 becomes −3; ceiling always goes up, so −2.9 becomes −2.

What's the difference between rounding and truncating?

Rounding picks the nearest value; truncating simply cuts off the extra digits. 7.89 rounds to 7.9 but truncates to 7.8.
