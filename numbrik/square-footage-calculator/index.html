Square Footage Calculator

Measure the area of rooms and shapes in square feet or square metres, add a waste allowance and estimate material cost for flooring, tile, carpet or turf.

How to use the Square Footage Calculator

  1. Choose a shape and feet or metres.
  2. Enter the measurements and how many identical areas you have.
  3. Add the material price and a waste percentage to see how much to buy and what it costs.

How it works

Area is length times width for a rectangle, π times the radius squared for a circle, and half the base times the height for a triangle. For an L-shaped or irregular room, split it into rectangles, work out each one and add them up.

Buy more than the exact area to allow for cuts and mistakes. About 10% extra is usual for straight layouts and 15% for diagonal or patterned tile. Measurements in inches can be divided by 12 to get feet.

Formula

Rectangle = length × width
Circle    = π × (diameter ÷ 2)²
Triangle  = base × height ÷ 2
1 m² = 10.764 sq ft     1 sq yd = 9 sq ft

Example

A 14 × 12 ft room is 168 sq ft (15.6 m²). With 10% waste you need 184.8 sq ft, and at $4.50 per sq ft the flooring costs about $831.60.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an L-shaped room?

Split it into two rectangles, measure each, calculate both areas and add them together.

How many square feet is a 12 × 12 room?

144 square feet, or about 13.4 square metres.

How do I convert square feet to square metres?

Divide by 10.764. 200 sq ft is about 18.6 m².

How much flooring should I buy?

Take the area with waste and round up to whole boxes. Keep a spare box for future repairs.
