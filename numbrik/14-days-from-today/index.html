How long is 14 days?

14 days is exactly 2 weeks, or about 0.5 months. That's 336 hours or 20,160 minutes.

14 days ago (today minus 14 days)

How it's counted

Today is day 0, tomorrow is day 1, and the date above is day 14. That's how most deadlines, return windows and notice periods are counted. If a rule says to include today, take one day off the result. The business-day date skips Saturdays and Sundays but not public holidays.

People look up 14 days from today for a 14-day return policy, a two-week notice period or a quarantine. For legal, visa or contract deadlines, check how the rule counts days, since some count calendar days and others count working days.

What is 14 business days from today?
