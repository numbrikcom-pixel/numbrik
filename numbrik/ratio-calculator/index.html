Ratio Calculator

Find the missing number in a proportion such as 3:4 = 12:?, and simplify any ratio to its lowest terms. Useful for scaling recipes, mixing concrete or paint, map scales, screen sizes and homework.

Loading calculator…

How to use the Ratio Calculator

  1. Enter the first ratio as A and B, for example 3 and 4 for 3:4.
  2. Enter C, the number you want to scale to.
  3. Read D, the missing value, plus the simplified ratio, the ratio as 1:n, and as a fraction and decimal.

How it works

Two ratios are in proportion when they are equal fractions: A/B = C/D. Cross-multiplying gives A × D = B × C, so the missing value is D = B × C ÷ A. That's all a proportion calculator does, but it's easy to put a number on the wrong side, so the answer is shown in full.

To simplify a ratio, both sides are divided by their greatest common factor. 18:24 has a GCF of 6, so it simplifies to 3:4. Decimal ratios are first multiplied by a power of ten, so 1.5:2 becomes 15:20 and then 3:4.

Formula

A : B = C : D   →   D = B × C ÷ A
Simplify: divide A and B by GCF(A, B)

Example

A recipe uses 3 cups of flour to 4 cups of milk. With 12 cups of flour you need 4 × 12 ÷ 3 = 16 cups of milk. The ratio 18:24 simplifies to 3:4, which is also 1:1.333.

Frequently asked questions

How do you solve a proportion?

Cross-multiply and divide. For 3:4 = 12:x, multiply 4 by 12 to get 48, then divide by 3 to get x = 16.

How do you simplify a ratio?

Divide both numbers by their greatest common factor. 10:25 has a GCF of 5, so it becomes 2:5.

What is the difference between a ratio and a fraction?

A ratio compares two quantities, part to part, such as 2 boys to 3 girls. A fraction compares a part to the whole, so the boys are 2/5 of the group.

How do I write a ratio as 1:n?

Divide both sides by the first number. 4:10 becomes 1:2.5.
