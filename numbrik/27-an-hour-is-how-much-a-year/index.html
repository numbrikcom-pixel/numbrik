$27 an hour is how much a year?

How it's calculated

A full-time year is usually counted as 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, which is 2,080 paid hours. $27 × 2,080 = $56,160. Paid holidays and paid vacation still count. If you take two weeks of unpaid leave, you work 50 weeks and earn $54,000.

A raise of $1 an hour adds $2,080 a year at full time. $27 an hour is 3.7 times the US federal minimum wage of $7.25. Many states and cities set a higher minimum.

$27 an hour after taxes

For a single filer in 2026 with no state income tax, $56,160 a year means about $4,559 in federal income tax, $3,482 in Social Security and $814 in Medicare. That leaves roughly $47,305 a year, $3,942.05 a month or $1,819.41 every two weeks. Use the take-home pay calculator to add state tax and 401(k) contributions. The pre-tax figures above apply to any currency paid by the hour, such as Canadian or Australian dollars; the after-tax estimate uses US rules.
