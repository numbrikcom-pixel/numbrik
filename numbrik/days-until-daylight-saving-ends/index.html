When is the end of daylight saving time?

In the US and most of Canada, daylight saving time ends at 2:00 a.m. on the first Sunday of November, when clocks go back one hour. The UK and the EU change on the last Sunday of October, and Australia's southern states on the first Sunday of April.

Is the end of daylight saving time a day off?

No. It's not a holiday, but you get an extra hour of sleep. Phones change automatically; set wall and car clocks back one hour.
