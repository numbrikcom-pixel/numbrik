How many calories do I need a day?

Most adults need about 1,600 to 3,000 calories a day. Your exact number is your BMR (from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation) times an activity factor of 1.2 to 1.9. Eat about 500 fewer a day to lose roughly a pound a week.

How to use the Calorie Calculator

  1. Choose your units and sex, then enter your age, height and weight.
  2. Pick the activity level that matches a normal week.
  3. Read your maintenance calories and the targets for gentle loss or gain.

How it works

The calculator first estimates your basal metabolic rate (BMR), the energy your body uses at rest, with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. It then multiplies BMR by an activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure (TDEE), which is roughly the calories that keep your weight stable.

A deficit of about 500 kcal a day is commonly linked to losing around 0.5 kg (1 lb) a week, though real results vary as the body adapts. Targets never go below 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men, because eating less than that without medical supervision is generally not advised. The results are estimates for healthy adults; if you are pregnant, under 18 or managing a health condition, ask a doctor or registered dietitian.

Formula

Men:    BMR = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5
Women:  BMR = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161
TDEE   = BMR × activity factor

Example

A 34-year-old woman, 5 ft 6 in and 150 lb, has a BMR of about 1,397 kcal. With light exercise (factor 1.375) her maintenance is about 1,920 kcal a day.

Tips for hitting your calorie target

  • Weigh foods for a week or two; portion sizes are where most estimates go wrong.
  • Prioritise protein and vegetables: they fill you up for fewer calories.
  • Count drinks: a large latte or a soda can be 200–300 calories.
  • Weigh yourself at the same time several days a week and look at the weekly average, not single days.
  • Recalculate after every 10 lb (4–5 kg) of change.

Frequently asked questions

What is TDEE?

Total daily energy expenditure: the calories you burn in a day from resting metabolism, digestion and movement combined.

How accurate is this?

Mifflin-St Jeor is one of the better-performing formulas in studies, but individuals can differ from the estimate by 10% or more. Track your weight for a few weeks and adjust.

Which activity level should I pick?

Choose by your typical week, not your best one. Most office workers who exercise a few times a week fit 'light' or 'moderate'.

Can teenagers use it?

No. Teenagers are still growing and have different needs, so they should get advice from a doctor.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

About 500 fewer than your maintenance calories a day to lose roughly 1 lb a week. Use the “Weight loss” line in the result. Don't go below 1,200 calories a day (women) or 1,500 (men) without medical supervision.

How many calories should a woman eat a day?

Most adult women need about 1,600 to 2,400 calories a day, depending on age, size and activity. A moderately active woman of 31 to 50 needs about 2,000.

How many calories should a man eat a day?

Most adult men need about 2,000 to 3,000 calories a day. A moderately active man of 26 to 35 needs about 2,600.

Do I eat back the calories I burn exercising?

Not separately: the activity level you choose already includes your usual exercise. Fitness trackers often overestimate calories burned, so be careful adding them on top.
