How it works

A US cup is 236.6 ml. Australia, New Zealand and Canada commonly use a 250 ml metric cup, so the same '1 cup' differs by about 5%; choose 'metric cup' for those recipes. Tablespoons differ too: a US tablespoon is about 14.8 ml, while an Australian tablespoon is 20 ml.

Cups measure volume and grams measure weight, so converting between them depends on the ingredient. One US cup of all-purpose flour weighs about 120–125 g, granulated sugar about 200 g and butter about 227 g.

Formula

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9
°F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32
K  = °C + 273.15

Example

1.5 US cups is 354.9 ml. An oven at 350°F is 176.7°C, which recipes usually round to 180°C (gas mark 4).

Frequently asked questions

How many ml are in a cup?

236.6 ml in a US cup and 250 ml in a metric cup used in Australia, New Zealand and Canada.

How many tablespoons are in a cup?

16 US tablespoons make a US cup. In Australia, with 20 ml tablespoons, a 250 ml cup is 12.5 tablespoons.

What is 180°C in Fahrenheit?

356°F. Recipes usually treat it as 350°F.

How many centimetres are in an inch?

Exactly 2.54 cm. A foot is 30.48 cm.
