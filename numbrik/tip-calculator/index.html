Tip Calculator

Work out the tip and how much each person pays when you split the bill.

How to use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill total.
  2. Pick a tip percentage or type your own.
  3. Enter how many people are paying and read each person's share.

How it works

The tip is the bill multiplied by the tip percentage. Add it to the bill and divide by the number of people to get each share.

Customs differ by country. In the US and Canada, 15–20% is usual at sit-down restaurants. In the UK a 12.5% service charge is often already on the bill, so check before adding more. In Australia and the Netherlands tipping is optional; rounding up or leaving around 5–10% for good service is common. In the US many people tip on the amount before sales tax.

Formula

Tip = bill × tip % ÷ 100
Each person = (bill + tip) ÷ people

Example

A $86.40 bill with an 18% tip is $15.55 in tip and $101.95 in total. Split three ways, each person pays $33.98.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a 20% tip on $50?

$10, for a total of $60. A quick way: move the decimal one place left to get 10% ($5), then double it.

Should I tip on the total before or after tax?

Etiquette guides in the US usually suggest tipping on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the full total. Either is accepted.

How do I split a bill unevenly?

Work out each person's share of the food and drinks, then apply the same tip percentage to each share.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

A service charge is added by the restaurant. If one is already on the bill, you usually don't need to tip again unless you want to.
