How it's counted

Today is day 0, tomorrow is day 1, and the date above is day 35. That's how most deadlines, return windows and notice periods are counted. If a rule says to include today, take one day off the result. The business-day date skips Saturdays and Sundays but not public holidays.

People look up 5 weeks from today for a 5 weeks plan, pregnancy milestones, a training block or a project timeline. For legal, visa or contract deadlines, check how the rule counts days, since some count calendar days and others count working days.
