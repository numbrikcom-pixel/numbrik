TDEE Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 13, 2026

Your TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) is how many calories you burn in a day, including exercise. Eat that many to keep your weight steady, fewer to lose weight, or more to gain. Enter your details and activity level to get yours.

What is TDEE and how is it calculated?

TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) is the calories you burn in a day. It's your BMR from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation multiplied by an activity factor: 1.2 for desk work up to 1.9 for very hard training.

How to use the TDEE Calculator

  1. Choose male or female and your units.
  2. Enter your age, height and weight.
  3. Pick the activity level that matches a normal week.
  4. Read your maintenance calories plus targets for losing or gaining weight.

How it works

The calculator first estimates your basal metabolic rate (BMR), the energy you'd use resting all day, with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. Studies have found it the most accurate of the common BMR formulas for most adults. It then multiplies BMR by an activity factor: 1.2 for a desk job with little exercise, 1.375 for light exercise, 1.55 for moderate, 1.725 for hard exercise most days and 1.9 for very hard training or physical work.

A pound of body fat holds roughly 3,500 calories, so eating about 500 calories below TDEE each day leads to roughly a pound a week of weight loss at first. Real results vary, and the loss slows as your weight drops, so recalculate every 10 pounds or so.

Formula

BMR (men) = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age + 5
BMR (women) = 10 × kg + 6.25 × cm − 5 × age − 161
TDEE = BMR × activity factor

Example

A 30-year-old man, 5 ft 10 in (178 cm) and 180 lb (81.6 kg), who exercises 3–5 days a week: BMR = 1,783 calories. TDEE = 1,783 × 1.55 = 2,763 calories a day. To lose about a pound a week he'd aim for around 2,263.

Frequently asked questions

What is TDEE?

Total daily energy expenditure: all the calories you burn in a day, from your resting metabolism, digesting food, everyday movement and exercise.

What's the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR is the energy you'd burn lying still all day. TDEE adds everything else you do, so it's always higher, usually 1.2 to 1.9 times BMR.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

A common starting point is 300–500 calories below your TDEE, which aims for about 0.5–1 lb a week. Avoid going below about 1,500 calories a day for men or 1,200 for women without medical advice.

Why am I not losing weight at my TDEE deficit?

Activity levels are often overestimated, food tracking misses calories, and water weight can hide fat loss for a week or two. Try the next activity level down and track for three to four weeks.
