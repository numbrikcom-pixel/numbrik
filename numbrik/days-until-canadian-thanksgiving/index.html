When is Canadian Thanksgiving?

Thanksgiving in Canada is the second Monday of October, about six weeks before US Thanksgiving.

Is Canadian Thanksgiving a day off?

Yes, in most provinces. It is a statutory holiday in most of Canada, but optional in the Atlantic provinces.
