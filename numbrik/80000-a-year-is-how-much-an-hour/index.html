$80,000 a year is how much an hour?

At 40 hours a week, $80,000 a year works out to $38.46 an hour before tax, or $6,666.67 a month.

How it's calculated

Divide the salary by the number of paid hours in a year. Full time is usually 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours, so $80,000 ÷ 2,080 = $38.46 an hour. If you regularly work more than 40 hours on a fixed salary, your real hourly rate is lower, as the table shows.

$80,000 a year after taxes

For a single filer in 2026 with no state income tax, expect about $8,770 in federal income tax, $4,960 in Social Security and $1,160 in Medicare, leaving roughly $65,110 a year or $5,425.83 a month. Add your state's tax and retirement contributions in the take-home pay calculator. The pre-tax figures apply to any currency; the after-tax estimate uses US rules.
