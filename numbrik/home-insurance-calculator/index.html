Home Insurance Calculator: Estimate Your Homeowners Premium

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 21, 2026

Estimate what homeowners insurance should cost before you get quotes. Choose your state, enter the cost to rebuild your home, pick a deductible and your claims history, and see an estimated yearly and monthly premium. UK homeowners see the latest average prices for buildings and contents cover.

How much is homeowners insurance?

About $2,772 a year on average in the US for $300,000 of dwelling coverage with a $1,000 deductible in 2026, from $948 in Vermont to $6,324 in Florida. In the UK, combined buildings and contents averaged £389 a year.

How to use the Home Insurance Calculator

  1. Choose the United States or the United Kingdom.
  2. Pick your state and enter your dwelling coverage, the cost to rebuild your home, or enter its size and a rebuild cost per square foot.
  3. Choose your deductible: the amount you pay toward each claim.
  4. Say how many claims you've made in the last five years.
  5. Read your estimated premium, monthly cost and cost per $1,000 of coverage.

How it works

The estimate starts from the average premium in your state for $300,000 of dwelling coverage with a $1,000 deductible, no recent claims and good credit, from November 2026 rate data. It then scales for your coverage amount: premiums rise more slowly than coverage, so $600,000 of cover costs about 1.7 times as much as $300,000, not twice as much. A $500 deductible adds about 7%; raising it to $2,500 saves about 9%, and $5,000 about 20%. One recent claim adds about 10%.

Dwelling coverage should equal the cost to rebuild your home from the ground up at today's labour and material prices, not its market value, which includes land. Ask your insurer for a replacement-cost estimate, or multiply the floor area by a local rebuild cost per square foot from a builder. Being underinsured is risky: many policies pay less on every claim if your cover is well below the rebuild cost.

Where you live matters most. Hurricanes, hail, tornadoes and wildfires push premiums in Florida, Oklahoma, Louisiana, Texas and the Plains far above average, while Vermont and New Hampshire pay a fraction as much. Standard policies don't cover flood or earthquake damage; those need separate cover. Your real quote also depends on the age of your roof, construction type, distance to a fire station, credit (in most states) and the insurer, so the result shows a ±25% range.

Formula

Estimate = state average ($300k) × coverage factor × deductible factor × claims factor
Coverage factor: $200k ×0.75, $400k ×1.25, $600k ×1.73, $1M ×2.68
Deductible: $500 ×1.07, $2,500 ×0.91, $5,000 ×0.80 · One claim ×1.10

Example

A home in Texas with $400,000 of dwelling coverage and a $1,000 deductible costs about $4,452 × 1.25 = $5,547 a year, or $462 a month. Raising the deductible to $2,500 cuts that to about $5,048.

UK and Canada

In the UK, the average combined buildings and contents policy cost £389 a year in July–September 2026, buildings-only cover £310 and contents-only cover £118, according to the Association of British Insurers. Mortgage lenders require buildings insurance; insure for the rebuild cost, which you can estimate with the BCIS calculator. In Canada, premiums vary widely by province and city, and overland flood and sewer backup are usually optional add-ons worth checking.

Frequently asked questions

How much is homeowners insurance per month?

About $231 a month on average in the US for $300,000 of dwelling coverage in 2026, from about $79 in Vermont to $527 in Florida.

How much homeowners insurance do I need?

Enough dwelling coverage to rebuild your home completely at today's costs, plus liability of at least $300,000. Market value doesn't matter, because it includes the land, which can't burn down.

Does a higher deductible save money?

Yes. Moving from $1,000 to $2,500 saves about 9% on average. Choose a deductible you could pay from savings the week after a loss.

Does homeowners insurance cover floods?

No. Standard policies exclude flooding. In the US, flood cover comes from the National Flood Insurance Program or private flood insurers, usually with a 30-day wait.

Is this calculator a quote?

No. It's an estimate from state averages. Roof age, construction, local risks, credit and the insurer all change the price, so compare quotes before you buy.
