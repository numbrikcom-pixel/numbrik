How do you convert between time zones?

Find each zone's offset from UTC on that date and add the difference. Eastern Standard Time is UTC−5 and India is UTC+5:30, so India is 10½ hours ahead: 9:00 AM EST is 7:30 PM IST.

How to use the Time Zone Converter

  1. Pick the date and time you want to convert.
  2. Choose the time zone it's in (From).
  3. Choose the time zone to convert to (To).
  4. Read the converted time, the day, and the time difference.

How it works

Every time zone is an offset from Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). The converter turns your time into UTC using the first zone's offset on that date, then into the second zone. It uses your browser's time zone database, so daylight saving time is handled automatically, including the weeks in March and autumn when the US and Europe change clocks on different dates.

In 2026, US daylight saving time ran from March 8 to November 1; in 2027 it runs from March 14 to November 7. The UK and EU switch on the last Sundays of March and October. India, China, Japan, Arizona and Hawaii don't use daylight saving time, so their difference from New York changes by an hour twice a year.

Formula

UTC time = local time − zone offset
Target time = UTC time + target offset
Example offsets: EST = UTC−5, PST = UTC−8, IST = UTC+5:30

Example

A call at 9:00 AM Pacific on Monday, November 9, 2026 is 12:00 PM Eastern, 5:00 PM in London and 10:30 PM in India. In summer, while the US is on daylight time, the same 9:00 AM PDT call would be 9:30 PM in India.

Frequently asked questions

What time is 12 PM EST in PST?

9 AM PST. Pacific time is three hours behind Eastern time all year, because both switch to daylight time on the same dates.

How many hours ahead is India from the US?

India (IST, UTC+5:30) is 10½ hours ahead of Eastern Standard Time and 13½ hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time. During US daylight time the gaps are 9½ and 12½ hours.

Is GMT the same as UTC?

For everyday use, yes: both are UTC+0. UTC is the scientific standard; GMT is also the UK's winter time zone. In summer the UK uses BST (UTC+1).

What's the difference between EST and EDT?

EST (UTC−5) is Eastern Standard Time, used in winter. EDT (UTC−4) is Eastern Daylight Time, used from March to November. Many people just say ET.
