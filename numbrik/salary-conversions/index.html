How to convert an hourly wage to a salary

Multiply the hourly rate by the hours you work in a week, then by 52. At 40 hours a week that's 2,080 hours a year, so a quick shortcut is to double the hourly rate and add three zeros: $20 an hour is about $40,000 a year (exactly $41,600). To go the other way, divide the salary by 2,080, or halve it and drop three zeros.

The 30-hour column is for part-time work. If you get paid holidays and vacation, the full 52 weeks applies. If you're paid only for the weeks you work, use the number of weeks you actually work instead, for example 50.

Before tax vs take-home pay

Every figure in the chart is gross pay, before income tax, Social Security, Medicare, retirement contributions and health insurance. Take-home pay is usually 70–80% of gross for a single US worker. For an estimate after 2026 federal tax, use the take-home pay calculator.
