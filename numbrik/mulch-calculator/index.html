Mulch Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 9, 2026

Work out how much mulch, topsoil or gravel you need for a garden bed. Enter the size and depth to get cubic feet, cubic yards and the number of bags to buy.

Loading calculator…

How do you calculate how much mulch you need?

Multiply the bed's length × width in feet by the depth in inches, divide by 12 for cubic feet, then by 27 for cubic yards. A 10 × 20 ft bed 3 inches deep needs about 1.85 cubic yards.

How to use the Mulch Calculator

  1. Choose a rectangle or circle bed, and feet or metres.
  2. Enter the size and the depth you want. 2–3 inches is typical for mulch.
  3. Read the cubic yards to order in bulk, or the number of 2 and 3 cubic foot bags.

How it works

The calculator finds the bed area, multiplies it by the depth to get the volume in cubic feet, then divides by 27 to get cubic yards, the unit landscape suppliers sell in. Bagged mulch is usually sold in 2 or 3 cubic foot bags. For several beds, add up the results.

Spread mulch 2–3 inches deep on flower beds and 3–4 inches on paths and around trees, keeping it a few inches away from trunks and stems. Fresh mulch settles, so ordering 5–10% extra is sensible. The same maths works for topsoil, compost, sand and gravel.

Formula

Volume (ft³) = length × width × depth (in) ÷ 12
Circle: 3.1416 × radius² × depth ÷ 12
Cubic yards = ft³ ÷ 27

Example

A 20 ft × 10 ft bed at 3 inches deep needs 20 × 10 × 3 ÷ 12 = 50 cubic feet, which is 1.85 cubic yards, or 25 two-cubic-foot bags (17 three-cubic-foot bags).

Bags per cubic yard

One cubic yard is 27 cubic feet: 13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet, or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet. Bulk delivery is usually cheaper once you need more than about 2–3 cubic yards.

Frequently asked questions

How much area does a yard of mulch cover?

At 3 inches deep, one cubic yard covers about 108 square feet. At 2 inches it covers 162 square feet.

How many bags of mulch are in a yard?

13.5 bags of 2 cubic feet, or 9 bags of 3 cubic feet.

How deep should mulch be?

2–3 inches for beds, 3–4 inches for paths. More than 4 inches can stop water and air reaching the soil.

Can I use this for gravel or soil?

Yes. The volume is the same for any loose material. Gravel is much heavier, so check the weight your vehicle can carry.
