Random & Game Tools

Fair random tools for games, classrooms and quick decisions: roll dice from d4 to d100, pick random numbers with or without repeats, or flip a coin up to 1,000 times. Every result comes from your browser's cryptographic random number generator.

All random & game tools

FunDice RollerRoll any number of dice, from d4 to d100.FunRandom Number GeneratorRandom whole numbers in any range, with or without repeats.FunCoin FlipFlip a coin once or up to 1,000 times.

Which one do I need?

Dice Roller
Roll virtual dice for board games, tabletop role-playing games and classroom games. Choose d4 to d100, roll up to 50 dice at once and add a modifier.
Random Number Generator
Pick one random number or up to 1,000 in any range, with or without repeats. Use it for raffles, games, classroom draws or choosing a winner.
Coin Flip
Flip a virtual coin for a quick heads-or-tails decision, or flip it up to 1,000 times and see the totals and the longest streak.
