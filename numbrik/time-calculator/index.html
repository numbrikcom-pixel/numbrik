Time Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 18, 2026

Add up a list of times like 2:45 + 1:30 + 0:50, subtract time, or find the clock time a number of hours and minutes from now. Results come as hours:minutes, decimal hours and total minutes.

How do you add hours and minutes?

Convert to minutes, add, and convert back: 2:45 + 1:30 = 165 + 90 = 255 minutes = 4 hours 15 minutes. For every 60 minutes, carry 1 hour.

How to use the Time Calculator

  1. Choose Add up durations to total a list of times, or Time of day ± hours to move a clock time forward or back.
  2. For durations, type one time per line as 2:45, 1:30:20 or 1h 15m. Put a minus sign in front of any time to subtract it.
  3. For a clock time, pick the start time, choose add or subtract, and enter the hours and minutes.
  4. Read the total, or the resulting time and whether it lands on another day.

How it works

Time is base 60, not base 10, so you can't add 2:45 and 1:30 like decimals. The calculator turns every time into seconds, adds or subtracts them, and turns the total back into hours, minutes and seconds. 2:45 + 1:30 is 165 + 90 = 255 minutes, which is 4 hours 15 minutes, not 3:75.

For a clock time, the calculator adds the duration to the start time and wraps past midnight, telling you if the result falls on the next day or the day before. 9:30 PM plus 4 hours 45 minutes is 2:15 AM the next day.

Formula

Seconds = hours × 3,600 + minutes × 60 + seconds
Total = sum of all times in seconds
Hours = total ÷ 3,600 (whole part)
Minutes = (remainder) ÷ 60

Example

2:45 + 1:30 + 0:50 − 0:20 = 165 + 90 + 50 − 20 = 285 minutes = 4 hours 45 minutes (4.75 hours). 9:30 AM + 8 h 45 min = 6:15 PM.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add hours and minutes together?

Add the minutes, and for every 60 minutes carry 1 hour. 2:45 + 1:30: 45 + 30 = 75 minutes, which is 1 hour 15 minutes; 2 + 1 + 1 = 4 hours, so the total is 4:15.

How do I subtract time?

Subtract the minutes first. If you don't have enough, borrow 1 hour as 60 minutes. 5:10 − 2:40: borrow so 5:10 becomes 4:70, then 4:70 − 2:40 = 2:30.

What time will it be in 8 hours?

Add 8 hours to the current time and subtract 12 (or 24) if you pass noon or midnight. Use Time of day ± hours above with your start time to get the exact answer.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 45 minutes is 0.75 hours, so 3 hours 45 minutes is 3.75 hours.
