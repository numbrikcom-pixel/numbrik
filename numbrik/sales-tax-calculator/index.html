Sales Tax Calculator

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 11, 2026

Add sales tax to a price, or remove it from a total to find the price before tax. Works for US sales tax, UK and Dutch VAT, Canadian GST and HST, and Australian GST.

How do you calculate sales tax?

To add tax, multiply the price by the rate and add it on. To remove tax from a total, divide by 1 plus the rate.

How to use the Sales Tax Calculator

  1. Choose whether you're adding tax to a price or removing it from a total.
  2. Enter the amount and the tax rate. The rate starts at a typical value for your region.
  3. Read the total, the tax and the price before tax.

How it works

To add tax, multiply the price by the rate and add it on. To remove tax from a total, divide by 1 plus the rate. Subtracting the percentage from the total gives the wrong answer: removing 20% VAT from £120 gives £100, not £96.

In the US there is no national sales tax. States, counties and cities set their own rates, so the combined rate depends on where you buy. In Canada, some provinces charge GST plus a provincial sales tax, and others charge a single HST.

Formula

Total = price × (1 + rate)
Price before tax = total ÷ (1 + rate)
Tax = total − price before tax

Example

A $100 item with 7% sales tax costs $107. Going the other way, a £120 total with 20% VAT has a price before tax of £100 and £20 of VAT.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate sales tax?

Multiply the price by the tax rate. 8% tax on $50 is $50 × 0.08 = $4, so you pay $54.

How do I work out the price before tax?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate. $54 ÷ 1.08 = $50.

Why can't I just subtract the percentage?

Because the tax was worked out on the smaller, pre-tax price. Subtracting 20% from a total that includes 20% VAT takes off too much.

Is sales tax the same as VAT?

Both are taxes on purchases. Sales tax is added at the till in the US, while VAT and GST are usually included in the shelf price.

How much is 8% sales tax on $35?

$2.80, for a total of $37.80. Multiply 35 × 0.08 = 2.80.

What is 120 plus 9.5% tax?

$131.40. The tax is 120 × 0.095 = $11.40.

How do I work out sales tax on a price?

Multiply the price by the tax rate as a decimal. For 7% tax on $140, 140 × 0.07 = $9.80, so the total is $149.80.
