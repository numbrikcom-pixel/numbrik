Hours Calculator

Find the time between two clock times, minus any unpaid break. You get the answer in hours and minutes and in decimal hours.

Loading calculator…

How to use the Hours Calculator

  1. Enter a start time and an end time.
  2. Add an unpaid break in minutes, or leave it at 0.
  3. Read the time worked in hours and minutes, decimal hours and total minutes.

How it works

The calculator turns both times into minutes after midnight, subtracts the start from the end and removes the break. When the end time is earlier than the start time, it assumes the period crosses midnight and adds 24 hours.

Decimal hours are the hours plus the minutes divided by 60. Timesheets and payroll software often ask for decimal hours, so 6 hours 20 minutes becomes 6.33.

Formula

Minutes = end − start − break   (add 1,440 if end is before start)
Decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60

Example

From 8:45 AM to 5:15 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes. Take off a 30-minute lunch and the time worked is 8h 00m, or 8.00 decimal hours.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is 9 to 5?

8 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 7.5 hours, and with a 1-hour lunch it is 7 hours.

How do I calculate hours across midnight?

Count from the start time to midnight, then add the time from midnight to the end. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 2 hours plus 6 hours, or 8 hours. The calculator does this for you.

What is 8 hours 20 minutes as a decimal?

8.33 hours, because 20 ÷ 60 = 0.333.

Should I use this or the time card calculator?

Use this page for one period of time. For a whole week with overtime and pay, use the time card calculator.
