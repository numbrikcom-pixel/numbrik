How do you calculate hours between two times?

Convert both times to minutes after midnight, subtract the start from the end, and take off any break. If the end time is earlier than the start, the period crosses midnight, so add 24 hours. Divide the minutes by 60 for decimal hours.

How to use the Hours Calculator

  1. Choose Between two times, Between two dates, or Add hours to a time.
  2. Enter the start and end times, or tap Now to use the current time. Add an unpaid break in minutes if you have one.
  3. Read the total in hours and minutes, the sentence answer you can copy, the decimal hours for a timesheet and the total minutes.

How it works

The calculator turns both times into minutes after midnight, subtracts the start from the end and removes the break. When the end time is earlier than the start time, it assumes the period crosses midnight and adds 24 hours.

Decimal hours are the hours plus the minutes divided by 60. Timesheets and payroll software often ask for decimal hours, so 6 hours 20 minutes becomes 6.33.

Formula

Minutes = end − start − break   (add 1,440 if end is before start)
Decimal hours = minutes ÷ 60

Example

From 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 9 hours (540 minutes, 9.00 decimal hours). Take off a 30-minute unpaid lunch and the time worked is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.50 decimal hours. Overnight, 7:00 PM to 7:00 AM is 12 hours.

How to calculate hours between two times

Step 1: Convert both times to 24-hour time

Add 12 to any PM hour except 12 PM: 4:30 PM becomes 16:30. 12 AM (midnight) becomes 0:00. The military time converter does this for you.

Step 2: Subtract the start time from the end time

Subtract the hours and the minutes separately: 16:30 − 7:30 = 9 hours 0 minutes. If the minutes go negative, borrow 60 minutes from the hours. If the end time is earlier than the start, the shift crosses midnight, so add 24 hours.

Step 3: Subtract unpaid breaks

Take off any unpaid lunch or rest break: 9 hours − 30 minutes = 8 hours 30 minutes.

Step 4: Convert the minutes to decimal hours

Divide the minutes by 60 for a timesheet: 30 ÷ 60 = 0.5, so 8 hours 30 minutes is 8.5 hours. Worked example: 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM with a 30-minute lunch is 9 hours − 30 minutes = 8.5 hours.

AM, PM, noon and midnight

Is 12 PM noon or midnight?

12 PM is noon and 12 AM is midnight, the start of the day. So 12 AM to 8 AM is 8 hours, and 8 PM to 12 AM is 4 hours. If you mean the very end of a day, enter 12:00 AM; the calculator treats it as the following midnight.

12-hour vs 24-hour (military) time

The 24-hour clock numbers the hours from 0 to 23, so there's no AM or PM: 2:00 PM is 14:00 and 11:30 PM is 23:30. It's used by the military, hospitals and most countries outside the US. The military time converter switches between the two.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is 9 to 5?

8 hours. With a 30-minute unpaid lunch it is 7.5 hours, and with a 1-hour lunch it is 7 hours.

How do I calculate hours across midnight?

Count from the start time to midnight, then add the time from midnight to the end. 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM is 2 hours plus 6 hours, or 8 hours. The calculator does this for you.

What is 8 hours 20 minutes as a decimal?

8.33 hours, because 20 ÷ 60 = 0.333.

Should I use this or the time card calculator?

Use this page for one period of time. For a whole week with overtime and pay, use the time card calculator.

How many hours is 8 to 5?

8:00 AM to 5:00 PM is 9 hours, or 8.5 hours after a 30-minute unpaid lunch.

How many hours is 7 PM to 7 AM?

7:00 PM to 7:00 AM is 12 hours. The calculator handles overnight shifts automatically.

How do I convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 45 ÷ 60 = 7.75 hours, which is the number most payroll systems use.

How many hours is 7:30 to 4:30?

7:30 AM to 4:30 PM is 9 hours, or 8.5 hours after a 30-minute unpaid lunch.

How many hours is a full-time shift?

There's no legal definition of full-time in US federal law, according to the U.S. Department of Labor, so employers set their own. A common full-time day is 8 hours, such as 9 AM to 5 PM, or 8 AM to 4:30 PM with a 30-minute unpaid lunch: 40 hours over a 5-day week.

How many hours is 8 AM to 4:30 PM with a 30-minute lunch?

8 hours. 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes, and taking off the 30-minute lunch leaves 8 hours, or 8.00 decimal hours.
