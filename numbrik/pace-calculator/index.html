How to use the Pace Calculator

  1. Enter the distance and choose miles or kilometres, or tap a race distance.
  2. Enter your time in hours, minutes and seconds.
  3. Read your pace, speed and race finish times at that pace.

How it works

Pace is time divided by distance. It's the usual way runners measure effort, because it tells you directly how long each mile or kilometre takes. Speed is the opposite: distance divided by time.

The race table shows how long each distance takes if you hold the same pace. Most people slow down over longer races, so treat the half marathon and marathon times as best cases. A common rule of thumb, Riegel's formula, predicts a little more than double the time each time the distance doubles, about 4% extra.

Formula

Pace = time ÷ distance
Speed = distance ÷ time
1 mile = 1.609344 km

Example

A 5K (3.107 miles) in 28:00 is a pace of 9:01 per mile or 5:36 per km, a speed of about 6.66 mph (10.7 km/h). Held for a full marathon, that pace finishes in about 3:56:18.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good running pace?

For recreational runners a pace of 9 to 12 minutes per mile (5:35 to 7:30 per km) is typical on easy runs. Racing pace is faster.

How do I convert pace per mile to pace per km?

Divide the pace per mile by 1.609. A 10:00 mile is 6:13 per km.

How long is a half marathon?

13.1 miles, or 21.0975 km. A full marathon is 26.2 miles, or 42.195 km.

Can I use it for walking or cycling?

Yes. Pace and speed work the same way for any activity.
