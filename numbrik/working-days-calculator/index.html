Working Days in a Year

By the Numbrik team · Tested against worked examples · Updated November 8, 2026

Count the working days in any year or month. There are 261 weekdays in 2026, and 250 working days after the 11 US federal holidays. Choose a year and add your own vacation days to see the days you'll actually work.

Loading calculator…

How to use the Working Days in a Year

  1. Pick a year.
  2. Choose whether to remove US federal holidays, and enter any vacation or PTO days.
  3. Read the working days and work hours for the year, with a month-by-month breakdown.

How it works

The calculator counts every Monday to Friday in the year, then removes the US federal holidays that fall on a weekday, using the observed date when a holiday lands on a weekend (Saturday holidays move to Friday, Sunday holidays to Monday). Your vacation days come off last.

A year has 52 weeks and one or two extra days, so it has 260, 261 or 262 weekdays. Private employers don't have to give federal holidays off, and many give fewer, so adjust the holiday setting to match your workplace. Work hours assume an 8-hour day.

Formula

Working days = weekdays − weekday holidays − PTO days
Work hours = working days × 8

Example

2026 has 261 weekdays. All 11 federal holidays fall on or are observed on a weekday, leaving 250 working days, or 2,000 hours at 8 hours a day. With 15 days of vacation you'd work 235 days. In 2027, New Year's Day 2028 falls on a Saturday and is observed on Friday, December 31, 2027, so 2027 has 249 working days.

Frequently asked questions

How many working days are in a year?

260 to 262 weekdays, depending on the year. After the 11 US federal holidays that's usually 249–251. In 2026 there are 250.

How many working days are in a month?

Usually 20 to 23 weekdays, about 21.7 on average. Holidays bring some months down to 19 or 20.

How many work hours are in a year?

At 40 hours a week, 2,080 hours before holidays and vacation. After federal holidays in 2026 that's 2,000 hours.

Does it include state holidays?

No. It removes US federal holidays only. Enter extra days off in the PTO box to account for state or company holidays.
