How much should I weigh for my height?

For most adults, a healthy weight gives a BMI of 18.5 to 24.9. The Devine formula gives a single ideal weight: men 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft, women 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch.

How to use the Ideal Weight Calculator

  1. Choose male or female and your units.
  2. Enter your height.
  3. Read the ideal weight range from the four formulas and your healthy BMI range.

How it works

Ideal body weight (IBW) formulas start from a base weight at 5 feet tall and add a fixed amount for each inch above that. The Devine formula (1974) was written to dose medicines and is still used in hospitals. Robinson and Miller (both 1983) refined it with population data, and the Hamwi formula (1964) is common in nutrition. They give slightly different answers, so the calculator shows the range.

The healthy BMI range is wider and more practical: any weight that gives a BMI from 18.5 to 24.9 is classed as a healthy weight by the World Health Organization. Neither method knows how much muscle you carry, so athletes can be healthy above both, and older adults may be better off slightly higher.

Formula

Devine (men) = 50 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft
Devine (women) = 45.5 kg + 2.3 kg per inch over 5 ft
Healthy BMI weight = 18.5 to 24.9 × height (m)²

Example

A man who is 5 ft 10 in has 10 inches over 5 feet. Devine: 50 + 2.3 × 10 = 73 kg (161 lb). Robinson gives 71 kg (157 lb), Miller 70.3 kg (155 lb) and Hamwi 75 kg (165 lb). His healthy BMI range is 129–174 lb.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I weigh for my height?

For most adults, any weight in the healthy BMI range of 18.5 to 24.9 is a reasonable target. For someone 5 ft 6 in tall that's about 115 to 154 lb.

Which ideal weight formula is most accurate?

None is accurate for everyone. Robinson and Miller are based on more recent data; Devine is the hospital standard for drug dosing. Use the range as a guide, not a single number.

Does age change my ideal weight?

The formulas don't use age. Some research suggests a BMI slightly above 25 carries little extra risk for adults over 65, so ask your doctor what suits you.

Why is my ideal weight lower than I expected?

These formulas ignore muscle mass and frame size. Muscular people often weigh more than the formula suggests and are still healthy. A body fat measurement tells you more.
