How do you convert minutes to decimal hours?

Divide the minutes by 60 and add them to the hours. 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + 45 ÷ 60 = 7.75 hours. To go back, multiply the decimal part by 60: 0.3 hours is 18 minutes.

How to use the Time to Decimal Calculator

  1. Choose time → decimal or decimal → time.
  2. Enter the hours and minutes (and seconds if you need them), or the decimal hours.
  3. Read the result, plus the total minutes and a 2-decimal figure for payroll.

How it works

To convert minutes to a decimal, divide them by 60. 45 minutes is 45 ÷ 60 = 0.75 hours, so 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours. Seconds are divided by 3,600. To go the other way, multiply the decimal part by 60: 8.4 hours is 8 hours and 0.4 × 60 = 24 minutes.

Payroll systems usually round decimal hours to two places, and some employers round time to the nearest 5 or 15 minutes (0.08 or 0.25 hours). A common mistake is reading 7.30 as 7 hours 30 minutes: in decimal it's 7 hours 18 minutes, while 7 hours 30 minutes is 7.5.

Formula

Decimal hours = hours + minutes ÷ 60 + seconds ÷ 3,600
Minutes = (decimal hours − whole hours) × 60

Example

A shift of 8 hours 20 minutes is 8 + 20 ÷ 60 = 8.33 hours (8.333…). At $22 an hour that's 8.333 × $22 = $183.33.

Frequently asked questions

What is 7 hours and 45 minutes in decimal?

7.75 hours, because 45 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.75.

What is 0.3 hours in minutes?

18 minutes. Multiply the decimal by 60: 0.3 × 60 = 18.

Is 7.30 hours the same as 7 hours 30 minutes?

No. 7.30 decimal hours is 7 hours 18 minutes. 7 hours 30 minutes is 7.5 hours.

How do I convert minutes to decimal for payroll?

Divide the minutes by 60 and round to two places. 10 minutes is 0.17, 15 minutes is 0.25, 20 minutes is 0.33 and 50 minutes is 0.83.
