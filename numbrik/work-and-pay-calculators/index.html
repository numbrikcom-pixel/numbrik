Work & Pay Calculators

Calculators for hourly and salaried workers in the US, Canada, Australia, the UK and the Netherlands. Add up a week of shifts with overtime, find the hours between two clock times, convert an hourly wage to a yearly salary, and estimate take-home pay after 2026 US federal tax, Social Security and Medicare.

Which one do I need?

Time Card Calculator
Enter start and end times for each day, take off unpaid breaks, and get total hours, overtime and gross pay for the week. Overtime rules follow the region you pick at the top of the page.
Hours Calculator
Find the time between two clock times, minus any unpaid break. You get the answer in hours and minutes and in decimal hours.
Hourly to Salary Calculator
Convert an hourly wage into yearly, monthly, biweekly and weekly pay, or work backwards from a salary to an hourly rate.
Take-Home Pay Calculator
Estimate your take-home pay per paycheck in the United States for 2026, after federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare, an estimated state tax and pre-tax deductions such as a 401(k).
