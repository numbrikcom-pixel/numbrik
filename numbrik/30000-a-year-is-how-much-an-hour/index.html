$30,000 a year is how much an hour?

At 40 hours a week, $30,000 a year works out to $14.42 an hour before tax, or $2,500.00 a month.

How it's calculated

Divide the salary by the number of paid hours in a year. Full time is usually 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, or 2,080 hours, so $30,000 ÷ 2,080 = $14.42 an hour. If you regularly work more than 40 hours on a fixed salary, your real hourly rate is lower, as the table shows.

$30,000 a year after taxes

For a single filer in 2026 with no state income tax, expect about $1,420 in federal income tax, $1,860 in Social Security and $435 in Medicare, leaving roughly $26,285 a year or $2,190.42 a month. Add your state's tax and retirement contributions in the take-home pay calculator. The pre-tax figures apply to any currency; the after-tax estimate uses US rules.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $30,000 a year per month?

$2,500.00 before tax. After estimated 2026 US federal tax and FICA for a single filer with no state tax, about $2,190.42.

How much is $30,000 a year every two weeks?

$1,153.85 before tax, or about $1,010.96 after estimated federal tax and FICA.
