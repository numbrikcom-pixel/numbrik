Money Calculators

Everyday money maths: work out a tip and split the bill, find percentages, discounts and percent change, see the monthly payment and total interest on a mortgage or loan with a yearly amortization chart, and estimate a car payment including sales tax, fees and a trade-in.

All money calculators

MoneyTip CalculatorTip amount and each person’s share.MoneyPercentage CalculatorPercent of, percent change, and what-percent.MoneyMortgage & Loan CalculatorMonthly payment, total interest and payoff by year.MoneyAuto Loan CalculatorMonthly car payment with down payment, trade-in and tax.MoneyCompound Interest CalculatorHow savings grow with compound interest and monthly deposits.MoneySales Tax CalculatorAdd or remove sales tax, VAT or GST from a price.MoneyCD CalculatorInterest earned on a certificate of deposit.

Which one do I need?

Tip Calculator
Work out the tip and how much each person pays when you split the bill.
Percentage Calculator
Three percentage calculators in one: find X% of a number, what percent one number is of another, and the percentage change between two values.
Mortgage & Loan Calculator
Find the monthly payment for a mortgage or any fixed-rate loan, with optional property tax, home insurance, PMI, HOA fees and extra payments. See the payoff date, the interest you'll pay and a yearly or monthly amortization schedule.
Auto Loan Calculator
Estimate your monthly car payment with the vehicle price, down payment, trade-in, sales tax and loan term all included.
Compound Interest Calculator
See how a starting amount and regular monthly deposits grow with compound interest, with a year-by-year table of deposits, interest and balance.
Sales Tax Calculator
Add sales tax to a price, or remove it from a total to find the price before tax. Works for US sales tax, UK and Dutch VAT, Canadian GST and HST, and Australian GST.
CD Calculator
See how much a certificate of deposit (CD) will earn. Enter the deposit, the APY and the term to get the interest earned and the balance at maturity, plus the same deposit over other common terms.
